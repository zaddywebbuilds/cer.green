# Writing conventions

## No dashes as punctuation

Never use an em dash (—) or an en dash (–) anywhere: not in visitor-facing
copy, not in component text, not in CMS field descriptions, not in comments,
and not in anything drafted for CER to send.

Use instead:

| Instead of a dash | Use |
| --- | --- |
| An aside or an appositive | a comma |
| Introducing a list or a definition | a colon |
| Two independent statements | a full stop |
| A parenthetical inside an already comma-heavy sentence | brackets |

Watch for the comma case specifically: when the sentence already contains a
list, a comma reads as another item in it. Use a colon there.

Hyphens in compound words (`AI-powered`, `6 to 12-month`) are fine. This rule
is about dashes used as punctuation between clauses.
